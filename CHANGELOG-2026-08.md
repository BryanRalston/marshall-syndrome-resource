## Claims verified (kept)

- GLP-1R protein on human NP cells: prior disc-cell papers (liraglutide/exenatide in vitro). Still not a Marshall clinical trial.
- Qin et al. *Cell Metabolism* 2026;38(3):582–597: ~17% MRI cartilage thickness increase is **real**, in **n=20 obese knee OA**, **semaglutide + hyaluronic acid vs HA alone**. Weight-independent mouse data exist. Not Marshall, not disc, not a reason to start a GLP-1 without a physician.
- Vyjuvek: first approved collagen gene therapy (FDA 2023). Follow-on approvals listed above.
- Allele-specific CRISPR/ASO/siRNA for dominant-negative collagens: still COL6/COL1/COL7, not COL11A1. Modarai et al. *Gene Therapy* 2025 review; Bolduc/COL6A1 2024; Genet Med 2025 COL6 exon-end variants + siRNA.
- FDA Plausible Mechanism draft exists (23 Feb 2026).
- n-Lorem is real and has expanded (50 treated patients Apr 2026). CNS/eye/kidney, not cartilage/disc.
- Carpenter et al. IPD meta: COL2A1 RD 47% vs COL11A1 28%; RR 1.78 (PMID 40825394).
- Camp et al. laser meta: 6.6% vs 36.0%; pooled RR 0.23 (PMID 40370211).
- Zhang et al. COL11A1 c.4069-1G>T minigene (Front Genet 2025).
- Velvin et al. Stickler psychosocial survey: 92% chronic pain (PMID 40891872).
- DiscGenics PIVOT: first patient 12 Jan 2026; NCT07254806; DSMB 9 Jun 2026 continue unmodified.
- Haploinsufficiency-is-safe rationale for allele silencing: unchanged, still supported by human null-carrier and cho/+ mouse data already on the site.
